Field collections are the foundation of my research on Drosophilidae. Most of my fieldwork has taken place in the Hawaiian Islands, where I have sampled native forests on Kauai, Oahu, Maui and Hawaii Island, alongside collecting trips in the continental US and Brazil. Below is a summary of where and when I have collected.
