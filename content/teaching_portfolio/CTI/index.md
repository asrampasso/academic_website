## Overview

The Center for Teaching Innovation (CTI) at Cornell University supports teaching across campus. Its Graduate Teaching Fellows program brings together experienced graduate students who design and deliver teaching workshops, institutes and events, serving as teaching ambassadors in their departments and across campus. I took part in the program for three years.

The CTI Fellows program is structured so that responsibility grows each year. First-year fellows develop and present teaching workshops, second-year fellows are paired with a first-year fellow to mentor them as they develop a workshop together, and a single senior fellow works with the program director, Dr. Derina Samuel, to lead the program and its fellows.

## Senior Graduate Lead Teaching Fellow (2024-2025)

![2024-2025](/2024-2025.png)

### Responsibilities
In my third year, as the program’s single senior fellow, I led a group of 20 fellows, working closely with Dr. Derina Samuel, who created and directs the program, to supervise their activities. I served on the selection committee, planned and led the retreats and the fellows’ and lead fellows’ meetings, reviewed draft workshops, planned the Institutes, organized and led the University-Wide conference, and coordinated program communications. I also collected and analyzed participant feedback and made recommendations for future iterations of the program.
